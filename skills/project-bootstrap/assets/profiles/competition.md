## Competition profile

Objective: maximize task completion, solution competitiveness, and final score under a hard time budget.

Default priority: explicit MUST deliverables > solution competitiveness > clarity/persuasiveness of results > necessary verification > research-style audit completeness.

- Explicit problem MUST items block freeze while FAIL/PARTIAL unless the responsible person consciously accepts the scoring loss; a limitation does not fulfill a missing deliverable.
- Prioritize a complete answer over validating an incomplete answer to perfection.
- If a rigorous method has weak competitive value, evaluate a practical pivot early.
- Evaluate the first complete solution from a reviewer/judge perspective before final freeze; keep this proportional to the stakes.
- Explain what was done, why, how good the results are, and improvements relative to relevant baselines.
- Keep limitations truthful, concise, and concentrated; avoid repeated defensive writing.
- Research defensiveness cannot replace actually answering the competition problem; formal claims still require real evidence.
- Treat the deadline as an optimization constraint when allocating implementation, writing, and verification effort.
